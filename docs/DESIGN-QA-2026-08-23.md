# SniperSight Phemex-style foundation — design QA

Date: 2026-08-23  
Reference viewports: desktop 1440 × 1024, mobile 390 × 844  
Scope: all 66 desktop and mobile screen, tab, sheet, and overlay states in the headless audit

## Findings

No P0, P1, or P2 visual defects remain after the final iteration.

Accepted P3 differences from the Phemex references:

- SniperSight retains its left evidence rail and safety-oriented information architecture because these surfaces explain automated decisions rather than reproduce an exchange order book.
- The phone header keeps a compact second row for equity, free risk, positions, orders, and HALT. This adds height versus Phemex but preserves book-safety facts at all times.
- JetBrains Mono remains on prices, quantities, timestamps, compact status codes, and aligned statistics. Navigation, prose, headings, forms, and actions use Inter.
- The desktop chart can remain in its loading state in an isolated headless capture. The mobile chart rendered with live product data, and the chart container, controls, and surrounding layout were verified at both target sizes.

## Fidelity review

- **Typography:** Inter now owns interface chrome and prose; mono is limited to machine-readable data. Heading shapes, case, tracking, and action labels follow the reference's restrained terminal style.
- **Spacing and rhythm:** desktop gutters, panel padding, tab rows, and the app rail are tighter. Phone chrome is reduced to 82px and the repeated Trade page heading is removed where the bottom navigation already supplies context.
- **Colour and surfaces:** neutral near-black surfaces, subtle dividers, small radii, and flat panels replace the former olive tint, scanline texture, glow, and raised controls. Saturated green is reserved for positive state and primary action.
- **Assets:** no fake imagery or replacement brand art was introduced. Existing SniperSight branding and authoritative chart output are retained.
- **Copy and data:** trading language, warnings, risk facts, and authoritative values are unchanged. The redesign is visual; it does not alter trade logic or compute values in the browser.

## Iterations completed

1. Replaced the decorative mono, scanline, glow, gradient, and large-radius treatment with the flat neutral foundation.
2. Reduced phone header height while retaining every safety fact, and removed redundant mobile Trade labeling.
3. Fixed the Stocks tab overflow cue so it is measured again when the user switches markets.
4. Made phone Evidence and Order sheets fully opaque and visually distinct from the chart beneath them.
5. Re-captured every desktop and phone state and repaired the two Spotter state captures so Observe and Capture were independently verified.
