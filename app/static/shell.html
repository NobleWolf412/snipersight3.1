Exchanges

read-only keys only —

Keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, tied to your user account, never written to the fact store or the log, and cannot be read back. A stored key does not enable live trading; that gate is separate and locked.

Risk

read-only

Sized by the engine, not editable here. Changing risk per trade would replay and re-size the entire forward record under the same engine version, which the versioning rule forbids.
