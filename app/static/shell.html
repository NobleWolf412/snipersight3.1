What is the bot allowed to do?

How the scanner works the five steps these rules control

It is always running. The scanner wakes every 60 seconds and walks the whole universe; one full pass takes roughly five minutes, so in practice it runs continuously. Run Scan on Command does not do anything extra — it runs the same pass immediately instead of waiting for the next wake-up.

  1. Rank the universe. Every symbol on the venue is sorted by 24-hour volume and the top slice is admitted. Controlled by: top n, min volume usd, enable perps.
  2. Warm up new symbols. A symbol needs 200 daily candles of history before it can be traded — long enough to map its structure. Until then it shows as WARMING and is deliberately excluded from sizing.
  3. Import closed candles. Only candles that have actually closed are imported, never the one still forming. That is why a fast move shows up as a drift alert before any engine reacts to it.
  4. Read the market. Structure, zones, regime and the rest run over the new candles and record what they find. This is what Market Weather shows: what condition each symbol is in.
  5. Wait for price to arrive. A tradeable condition is not a trade. A setup only appears when price returns to one of that symbol's zones and confirms there — then it lands on the Setup Deck. Controlled by: the playbook switches above.

So an empty Setup Deck beside a busy Market Weather is the normal state, not a fault. Weather is necessary and nowhere near sufficient: a symbol can sit in a tradeable regime for days without price ever coming back to a zone. Quiet days are the design.

Nothing here places a real order. Arm writes to your own paper book, kept separate from the strategy's record so hand-picked trades are never counted as engine edge. Live execution is locked.

Venue & Credentials —

Keys are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, tied to your user account. They are never written to the fact store, the log, or git, and no endpoint can read one back — the app can only tell you whether one is set. Use read-only keys. A stored key does not enable live trading; that gate is separate and still locked.

Risk —

These are the live numbers the risk authority sizes with, read from the engine. They are not editable here yet — and not because the field is missing. Changing risk per trade re-sizes the entire forward record, because the account is recomputed by replaying every setup. Doing that in place would rewrite your paper track record under the same engine version, which is exactly what the versioning rule forbids. The envelope is also coupled: the total cap holds two concurrent trades and the daily halt is about three stop-outs, so one value cannot move alone.

Strategies & Universe unsaved
Changing a rule starts a new forward window, because a record that spans two configurations can't tell you which one produced which result. Nothing is deleted.
Guardrails —

Every one of these blocks NEW entries only. Open positions still settle — refusing to close a position is not a safety feature, it is just a different way to lose control of it. Live order submission stays locked regardless.
