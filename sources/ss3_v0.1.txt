SNIPERSIGHT3
Product Constitution and v0 System Specification
A governance and engineering foundation for a deterministic, explainable market-structure platform
Version 0.1
Prepared for product design and implementation planning

Executive Summary
SniperSight3 is defined as a market-structure intelligence, strategy-research, risk-management, and trade-execution platform. This document establishes the non-negotiable rules for the product and specifies the first development milestone: a replayable, inspectable, deterministic market-structure engine with no live trading.
The development gate is simple: the system must be able to explain what it knew, when it knew it, how it classified the market, and which algorithm version produced the result.
1. Purpose
The constitution prevents the platform from becoming a collection of unrelated indicators, a constantly changing scoring system, an untestable AI black box, a backtester that uses future information, or a live system whose past decisions cannot be reproduced.
Any component that violates these rules must be redesigned before it can influence real capital.
2. Product Identity
SniperSight3 is a market-structure intelligence, strategy-research, risk-management, and trade-execution platform.
It is not primarily a signal service, chart indicator, social-trading platform, self-learning autonomous bot, guaranteed-profit system, or replacement for risk management.
3. Core Operating Layers
Layer 1 - Market observations
Direct measurements: OHLCV, trades, order-book data, funding, open interest, and exchange account events. No interpretation occurs here.
Layer 2 - Derived market facts
Deterministic interpretations: swings, protected levels, BOS, CHoCH, zones, liquidity clusters, regimes, and volatility states.
Layer 3 - Trading hypotheses
Testable strategy claims, such as whether a bearish break and retest has favorable expectancy under defined conditions.
Layer 4 - Trade intents
Strategy-generated requests containing direction, entry, invalidation, targets, expiration, and requested account risk.
Layer 5 - Executable orders
Risk-approved venue instructions containing final quantity, price, protection, time-in-force, and an idempotent client order identifier.
No component may skip layers. A strategy cannot write directly to the exchange.
4. Determinism Principle
Given the same market data, algorithm version, configuration, and calculation time, SniperSight3 must produce the same result.
This applies to candles, features, swings, structure, zones, regimes, setups, strategy decisions, position sizing, risk decisions, and simulated execution.
Randomized ML algorithms must record seeds, data versions, and environment versions.
5. Causality and Look-Ahead Prevention
A candle is not final until it closes. Developing-candle observations must be labeled provisional.
A swing requiring candles to its right is not confirmed until those candles close. The system records market time, detection time, and confirmation time.
Historical replay may not display facts before confirmation. Model features must use only information available at prediction time.

7. Versioning Constitution
Everything that can affect a result must be versioned: data normalization, candle aggregation, swing detection, structure classification, zone logic, liquidity logic, regime logic, strategy logic, risk rules, sizing, execution simulation, fees, slippage, features, models, and trade-management policies.
A version becomes immutable once used in a recorded backtest, paper trade, or live trade. Editing creates a new version.
8. Auditability Constitution
Every trade must be reconstructable from the available data through the final outcome.
What market data was available?
What market facts existed?
Which algorithms and versions created them?
Which strategy version evaluated them?
Why did the strategy create a trade intent?
What risk rules were applied?
Why was the trade approved, modified, or rejected?
What orders and fills occurred?
How was the position managed?
Why did it close, and what was the net result?
9. Risk Constitution
Risk control is independent from strategy logic. Strategies request risk; the risk authority approves, reduces, or rejects it.
Position sizing
Maximum risk per trade
Daily and total drawdown limits
Portfolio, directional, and correlated exposure
Leverage and concurrent-position limits
Data, exchange, and account health
Kill-switch state
Stops should represent structural invalidation. Stops may tighten after entry but may not be widened to increase the originally approved loss.
10. Execution Constitution
Exchange state is authoritative.
Order operations must be idempotent and safe to retry.
Every filled position requires verified protection.
New entries remain disabled after restart until reconciliation completes.
UNKNOWN order state is critical and must be resolved.
11. Strategy Constitution
Every strategy must define eligible instruments, timeframes, context, setup, confirmation, entry, invalidation, targets, management, expiration, cancellation, re-entry, concurrency, and data-quality requirements.
Undocumented discretion such as “take it when the chart looks clean” is not acceptable.
12. Machine-Learning Constitution
Initially, ML may rank valid setups, estimate outcome probabilities, retrieve similar historical contexts, detect drift, and identify anomalies.
It may not change leverage or risk.
It may not remove or widen stops.
It may not bypass deterministic requirements.
It may not promote or deploy itself.
13. Human-Control Constitution
The dashboard must clearly show automation status, active account, strategy version, eligible instruments, open risk, pending orders, positions, protection state, and system health.
Disable a strategy or instrument
Cancel pending entries
Close a position
Disable all automation
Trigger emergency shutdown
14. Research Constitution
Each research run records strategy version, algorithms, instruments, period, data source/version, parameters, fees, funding, slippage, fills, starting capital, risk rules, software commit, and run timestamp. Results are immutable.
15. Performance-Reporting Constitution
Trade count and win rate
Average win, average loss, expectancy, and profit factor
Maximum drawdown and time underwater
Fill rate, missed-entry rate, fees, funding, and slippage
Performance by regime, instrument, direction, and dataset split
In-sample, validation, out-of-sample, paper, and live results must remain separate.
16. Scope Constitution
v0 explicitly excludes live execution, ML-controlled trading, strategy marketplaces, options, forex, multi-exchange routing, social sentiment, news prediction, copy trading, reinforcement learning, mobile apps, public billing, and indicator sprawl.

20. Initial Swing Methodology
Micro swing
Five-candle fractal using two closed candles on each side. Intended for fine structure and entry analysis.
Local swing
A confirmed micro swing producing a reversal of at least a configurable volatility-adjusted amount. Initial research default: 0.75 ATR.
Structural swing
A local swing that breaks an existing structural level, produces meaningful displacement, or forms a significant directional leg.
Major swing
Macro structure used primarily on weekly, daily, and possibly 4H charts.
21. Structural Vocabulary
A CHoCH indicates transition risk; it does not by itself prove a complete trend reversal.
22. Initial Structural-Break Rule
The level belongs to a confirmed structural swing.
A closed candle finishes beyond the level.
The close exceeds the level by a minimum tolerance.
A wick alone does not confirm the break.
Initial tolerance: the greater of a small tick buffer or 0.05 ATR. Displacement, body size, relative volume, close location, follow-through, and reclaim behavior should be recorded as evidence before becoming required filters.
23. Zone Specification
Sources: structural swing clusters, repeated reactions, consolidation before displacement, prior daily highs/lows, and prior weekly highs/lows.
Lifecycle: FRESH, TOUCHED, TESTED, WEAKENED, BROKEN, FLIPPED, INVALIDATED.
Broken zones remain stored; their state changes instead of deleting history.
24. Liquidity Specification
Equal highs/lows within an initial 0.10 ATR tolerance
Three or more nearby swing highs/lows
Previous daily and weekly extremes
Sweep, rejected sweep, and accepted sweep events
A sweep requires price to trade beyond a level and return through it. A rejected sweep additionally requires a close back inside or a lower-timeframe structural reversal.

1H: TRANSITION - bullish correction - retest developing
15m: RANGE - local equal highs - liquidity cluster formed
